I recently presented our paper, **“Prescriber Influence on the Adoption of New Medicines: Evidence from Movers in Australia,”** at the Department of Economics, University of Technology Sydney.

The paper is joint work with [Kadek Darmawan](https://researchers.mq.edu.au/en/persons/kadek-hendra-darmawan/), [Jonas Fooken](https://researchers.mq.edu.au/en/persons/jonas-fooken/), and [Smriti Raichand](https://researchers.mq.edu.au/en/persons/smriti-raichand/) at the Macquarie University Centre for Health Economy, and is part of Kadek’s PhD thesis.

Many thanks to the Department of Economics for the invitation, and in particular to [Ali Vergili](https://sites.google.com/view/alivergili/) for hosting me. I received very nice feedback from everyone about the empirical design and potential extensions, as well as ideas for new papers :)
